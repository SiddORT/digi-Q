# 1. PRODUCT OBJECTIVE

ClinicFlow is a multi-clinic doctor appointment and queue management platform.

The system must allow:

1. Doctors to register and manage their profiles.
2. Doctors to create/manage clinics.
3. Doctors to define clinic-wise availability.
4. Doctors to configure consultation hours.
5. Doctors to configure queue/token settings.
6. Receptionists to manage appointments and walk-ins.
7. Receptionists to search existing patients.
8. Receptionists to create new patients.
9. Receptionists to book appointments.
10. Receptionists to manage live queues.
11. Patients to book appointments.
12. Patients to book through QR/link-based flows.
13. Patients to receive OTP verification.
14. Patients to view upcoming appointments.
15. Patients to view live queue status.
16. Doctors to call/manage patients from their queue.
17. Administrators to manage doctors.
18. Administrators to manage clinics.
19. Administrators to manage users.
20. Administrators to manage masters/configuration.
21. Administrators to view platform-level information.
22. The system to maintain proper role-based access control.
23. The system to maintain appointment and queue history.
24. The system to work across multiple clinics and branches.
25. The system to be responsive for desktop, tablet and mobile.

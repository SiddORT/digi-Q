name: Communication usage boundary
description: Product intent for observed communication reporting and future subscription work
---

Communication Usage is an observed-activity report, not the billing authority
for a future subscription-packaging project. Provider acceptance is not
confirmed delivery, provider credit consumption or billable SMS segments.
Do not silently reinterpret existing totals as any of those measures.

**Why:** The user explicitly commissioned this report to inform later packaging
without implementing billing-grade metering, and acknowledged incomplete
security transports, best-effort history and unrecoverable earlier purges.

**How to apply:** Keep coverage gaps and partial/unattributed history visible.
Any future billing authority needs a separately agreed capture/reconciliation
contract. Keeping all-time history must not weaken authorized privacy erasure or
revive erased records through legacy fallback.

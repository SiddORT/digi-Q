---
name: Manual email receipt boundary
description: Meaning and scope of the approved user-confirmed test-email receipt
---

Manual test-email receipt is an administrator assertion about one test, not
automatic inbox verification, sender authorization certification or future
delivery assurance. Preserve the original provider outcome independently.

**Why:** The user approved an “I received the test email” confirmation with a
timestamp after discussing the limits of SMTP connection checks.

**How to apply:** Keep receipts bound to the actual test, confirming actor and
SMTP configuration. Label historical configuration receipts explicitly; never
turn confirmation into another send, global readiness or notification activation.

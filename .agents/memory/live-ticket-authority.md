---
name: Live-ticket authority
description: Public status sharing must not replace existing guest receipt management or claim a guest booking.
---
Patients see estimated wait and suggested arrival, not configured per-patient duration or a promised consultation time.

**Why:** The user approved arrival guidance as an estimate and explicitly distinguished it from booking an exact consultation time.

**How to apply:** Keep uncertainty visible across online, printed and emailed tickets; use honest unavailable guidance when delays cannot be determined.

The public shared status link is read-only. Existing guest receipt management remains a separate, more powerful authority; signing in must not automatically claim a guest booking.

**Why:** The user explicitly required preserving existing guest receipt capability management and excluded new guest-account claiming flows.

**How to apply:** Never replace guest receipt exports or permitted management with a login requirement. Only the server-verified booking owner gains personal ticket details and management on the public status page.

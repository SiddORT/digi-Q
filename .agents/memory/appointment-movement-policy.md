---
name: Appointment movement policy boundaries
description: Approved meaning of Shift Time versus Reschedule and limits on related workflow changes
---

Shift Time means a strictly later pending position in the same session, including
just one place later. Reschedule means a different session/day. Neither promises
a consultation time or lets a patient obtain immediate-next priority.

**Why:** The user explicitly approved one-position-later movement and distinguishes
queue order from the original token identifier.

Ordinary appointment mobility before/during the source session must not broaden
staff-account care handoff eligibility or notification delivery authorization.

**Why:** The approved rescheduling cutoff change is deliberately narrower than
staff removal and live patient communication policies. Those retain their
independent safeguards.

**How to apply:** Treat call release as an explicitly confirmed staff operation,
not a false absence report. Preserve the stricter account-handoff review boundary
when reusing ordinary appointment services. Development verification uses
fictional records and fake transports, never live patient delivery.

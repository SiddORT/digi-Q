The proposed solution is a web-based, multi-doctor Clinic Queue & Patient Appointment Management System designed to reduce physical waiting queues, prevent patients from arriving when the day's capacity is already full, and minimize repeated calls to the receptionist asking about waiting times.
The system connects four key users — Doctors, Patients, Receptionists, and Admins — through one centralized platform.
How the solution works
Doctors configure their weekly or monthly availability, including their consultation hours and the maximum number of patients/tokens they can accommodate during each consultation session. Since consultation durations can vary, the system will not promise fixed appointment times. Instead, patients reserve a place in the doctor's consultation queue.
Patients can access the booking system through a web link shared by the receptionist or by scanning a QR code displayed by the clinic. After verifying their mobile number through OTP, they can select their preferred doctor, view the doctor's available consultation sessions, see the remaining capacity and indicative waiting situation, and reserve an appointment.
Once booked, the patient receives a booking confirmation and token/booking reference through SMS.
When the patient reaches the clinic, they show their token to the receptionist. The receptionist validates the booking and checks the patient in, after which the patient becomes part of the active consultation queue.
The receptionist manages the queue in real time, while the doctor can see the patients waiting, the patient currently being consulted, completed consultations, no-shows, and the next patient.
Live Queue & Waiting-Time Management
One of the core features is the live queue.
For example, a patient could see:
Your Token: A-18
Currently Serving: A-14
Patients Ahead: 4
Estimated Wait: ~35 minutes
The patient can check this information remotely through their phone instead of repeatedly calling the clinic.
The system should also automatically send an SMS approximately 20 minutes before the patient's token is expected to be called, allowing the patient to reach the clinic closer to their actual consultation time.
The waiting time is an estimate, not a guaranteed appointment time, because consultation duration will naturally differ from patient to patient.
Multi-Doctor Clinic Support
The system is designed for a clinic with multiple doctors. Each doctor maintains their own availability, consultation sessions, capacity and queue.
For example:
Dr. A: 9:00 AM–1:00 PM | 40 patients
Dr. B: 10:00 AM–2:00 PM | 30 patients
Dr. C: 5:00 PM–8:00 PM | 25 patients
Patients choose the doctor they want to consult, while the receptionist can manage appointments and queues for all doctors from one dashboard.
Role-wise functionality
Doctor: Manages availability and patient capacity, views today's appointments and queue, tracks waiting patients, starts/completes consultations, and reviews basic patient/queue information.
Receptionist: Manages bookings, shares booking links, assists patients who cannot book digitally, validates tokens, checks patients in, handles walk-ins, updates patient statuses and operates each doctor's live queue.
Patient: Books through QR/link, verifies their mobile number through OTP, selects a doctor and available consultation session, receives confirmation, views upcoming appointments and tracks their live token/waiting position on the day of consultation.
Admin: Manages doctors, clinic information, users, permissions, platform settings and overall reporting.
Notifications
SMS becomes an important part of the patient experience. At minimum, the system should support OTP verification, booking confirmation, appointment/session reminders, queue-near notifications (~20 minutes before expected turn), cancellation/rescheduling notifications, and relevant queue updates.
Overall patient journey
QR / Booking Link → Mobile OTP → Select Doctor → View Availability & Remaining Capacity → Reserve Appointment → Receive Confirmation → Monitor Queue → Receive “Your Turn is Approaching” SMS → Arrive at Clinic → Receptionist Validates Token & Checks In → Join Active Queue → Consultation → Completed.
The fundamental idea is to change the clinic experience from “come to the clinic and wait until your turn” to “reserve your place, know the queue status remotely, and arrive closer to when the doctor can actually see you.”
This addresses the three primary problems: overcrowding at the clinic, patients arriving after the day's capacity is already full, and constant calls to reception asking how long the wait is.
